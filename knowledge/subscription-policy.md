# Subscription and Plan Change Policy

## Plan Tiers

| Plan | Billing | Minimum term | Support tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | Monthly in arrears | none | Standard |
| Business | Monthly in advance | 3 months | Priority |
| Enterprise | Monthly in advance | 12 months | Named contact |

The direction of billing — in advance or in arrears — is the single most
frequently misread difference between plans, and it is the source of most
"we were charged before we used the service" reports. On Business and Enterprise,
being invoiced on the first day of the period is correct behaviour, not an error.

## Upgrades

An upgrade takes effect immediately. The customer is charged pro-rata for the
remainder of the current period at the new rate, less the unused portion of the
old rate. This produces two line items on one invoice: the pro-rated remainder
and a credit for the unused old plan.

Two line items at different amounts on one invoice are **not** a duplicate
charge. The duplicate-detection criteria require matching amounts; a pro-ration
pair never matches.

## Downgrades

A downgrade takes effect at the **start of the next billing period**. No credit
is issued for the remainder of the current period, because the higher-tier
service remains available until the period ends.

A customer who downgrades and expects an immediate credit is expecting something
this policy does not provide. Explain the effective date rather than issuing a
refund.

## Cancellation

| Cancelled | Effect |
|---|---|
| Within 14 days of first purchase, no usage | Full refund |
| Within 14 days, with usage | Refund less usage at the standard rate |
| After 14 days | No refund; service continues to the end of the paid period |
| Mid-term on an Enterprise contract | Early-termination fee per contract; no refund |

Cancellation does not stop an invoice already issued. The final invoice covers
the period during which the account was active.

## Reactivation

A cancelled account reactivated within 30 days resumes the same subscription
identifier and retains its data. After 30 days, reactivation creates a new
subscription and the prior data may have been purged per the retention schedule.

Reactivation within 30 days does not refund or credit the period during which the
account was cancelled. The customer was not being charged during that period, and
there is nothing to return.

## Seat Count Changes

Handled per plan: Standard and Business adjust at the next period boundary;
Enterprise adjusts pro-rata in both directions, per
[enterprise-billing.md](enterprise-billing.md).
